Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Return Window

Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery, provided they are unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.

How to Start a Return

  • Contact us via the Contact page with your Order ID and the reason for return
  • We'll confirm eligibility and arrange pickup or provide return instructions
  • Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked as final sale
  • Personal care and hygiene products that have been opened
  • Items damaged due to misuse after delivery

Refunds

Since MIZANORA operates on Cash on Delivery, refunds for approved returns are issued as store credit or via bank transfer, whichever you prefer — we'll confirm details with you directly during the return process.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund at no extra cost to you.

Questions

Reach out via our Contact page for any return or refund questions.
