How to Spot Fake Online Shops in Pakistan: 12 Warning Signs

Learn 12 warning signs of fake online shops and scam sellers in Pakistan, and what to do to protect your money before you order.

Social media has made it easy for anyone to open an online shop in a few minutes, and that includes people who want to cheat. Fake shops usually collect an advance payment and then disappear, or send a very different product from what was shown. Here are twelve warning signs, and what to do about them.

The 12 warning signs

1. The page is very new

A page created a few days ago with a few posts and many fast followers deserves caution. Look at when it started posting.

2. Prices are far too low

If a famous brand is sold for a tiny fraction of its normal price, either the item is fake or there is no item. Compare the price with other sellers.

3. The photos look stolen

Search a product photo using Google Lens or a reverse image search. If the same photo appears on many unrelated pages, it is not the seller's own stock.

4. The seller refuses a real video

Ask for a short video of the exact item, held in hand, with today's date or your name written on paper. Honest sellers can do this easily.

5. They demand full advance payment

Be careful when someone asks for the full amount immediately and refuses cash on delivery. Some trusted sellers do require advance payment, so judge this together with the other signs.

6. The payment account name does not match

If you are asked to send money to a personal account with a different name from the shop, ask why. Check the account title before paying.

7. Pressure and false urgency

Messages like "last piece, pay in 10 minutes" are used to stop you from thinking. Real shops give you time.

8. Vague answers

If the seller cannot tell you the material, size or delivery time, they probably do not have the product.

9. No contact details

A real business gives a working phone number and links to its accounts. Be careful with shops that only reply through one hidden account.

10. Comments are turned off or look fake

All comments in the same style, or comments disabled everywhere, can mean the page is hiding complaints. Look for natural, different comments.

11. Bad reviews exist elsewhere

Search the shop name and phone number with the word "scam" or "complaint". Many cheated customers post warnings.

12. They ask for private information

Nobody needs your card PIN, OTP codes, or CNIC photos to sell you a product. Never share them.

What to do before you order

  • Message the seller and ask detailed questions.
  • Ask for a real video of the product.
  • Prefer cash on delivery when it is available.
  • Save the whole chat as proof.
  • If you must pay in advance, pay a small amount first, or use a method with a record you can show.

What to do if you are cheated

  1. Stop all payments immediately.
  2. Take screenshots of the chat, the payment receipt, the page and the seller's number.
  3. Report the page on the platform (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp).
  4. Inform your bank or wallet provider (JazzCash, Easypaisa or your bank) as soon as possible.
  5. Consider reporting to the relevant cybercrime authority in Pakistan. Check the official website for the current complaint process.

This is general advice and not legal advice. For serious losses, speak to a lawyer.
