Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MIZANORA ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

  • Order information: name, phone number, delivery address, city, and province, provided at checkout.
  • Account information: if you create an account, your name and email address.
  • Optional information: email address at checkout, order notes, and product reviews you choose to submit.

We do not collect payment card details — MIZANORA currently operates on a Cash on Delivery basis only.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To communicate with you about order status
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our products and services

How We Protect Your Information

Your data is stored using Firebase, with access controlled by strict security rules — customers can only access their own order and account data, and administrative functions are restricted to verified admin accounts.

Your Choices

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or request account deletion by contacting us at the email listed on our Contact page.

Guest Checkout

You are never required to create an account to place an order. Guest orders are looked up only via a matching Order ID and phone number.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of MIZANORA after changes means you accept the revised policy.
